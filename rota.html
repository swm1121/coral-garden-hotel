Northern Mariana Islands

Rota,
The Quiet One

The Island

What to expect
before you arrive.

Rota is a small island — about 85 square kilometers — in the Northern Mariana Islands chain, roughly 80 km north of Guam. Population around 1,500. One main town, a few roads, clear water, and minimal tourist infrastructure.

There are no cruise ships, no resort corridors, and no peak-season crowds. What there is: some of the clearest water in the Pacific, a resident diving community, good hiking, and a genuinely unhurried pace. Travelers who have been to Guam, Saipan, or Hawaii and found them overrun tend to find Rota exactly right.

WiFi is available at the hotel. Rental cars are available at the airport — book in advance, as inventory on-island is limited. Most things a traveler needs in Songsong are within walking distance of Coral Garden.

What to Do

Diving & Snorkeling

Rota's reefs are among the Pacific's least-dived and most intact. The Shoun Maru wreck, coral channels, and blue holes are all accessible. Visibility regularly hits 30+ meters. Several operators work out of Songsong, all within walking distance of the hotel.

Hiking & Nature

Mt. Taipingot, Tonga Cave, and the Bird Sanctuary offer trails from easy to strenuous. The Rota white-eye — a small bird found nowhere else on Earth — lives in the forested interior. Good reason to bring binoculars.

Songsong Village

Rota's only town. Walkable, local, and slow. Restaurants, the harbor, the market, and cultural sites are all accessible on foot from Coral Garden. Nothing is far. Nothing feels rushed.

Historical Sites

Layered history across Chamorro, Spanish, German, Japanese, and WWII eras. The As Nieves Latte Stone Quarry and the Rota Cultural Museum are the two worth prioritizing. Plan a half-day for each.

Fishing

Offshore fishing for mahi-mahi, wahoo, and tuna. Charters work out of the harbor, 10 minutes from the hotel on foot. Good year-round, stronger in the drier months.

Beaches & Swimming

Teteto Beach, Sasanhaya Bay, and the shoreline behind the hotel. Uncrowded, warm, and genuinely clear. No lifeguards, no amenity vendors. Just water.

Getting Here

By Air

Fly into Rota International Airport (ROP). Flights operate from Guam (~30 min) and Saipan. Check Island Air CNMI for current schedules. Guam is the main international gateway.

From Guam

Guam (GUM) has the most international connections. Total travel time from the US mainland is typically 18–22 hours depending on routing. From Asia, considerably less.

On the Island

The airport is 15 minutes from Songsong by car. Rental cars are available on-island — book ahead, as inventory is small. Within Songsong, most things our guests need are walkable from the hotel.

Practical Notes for Travelers

Rota operates at its own pace. Services and shops have island hours. Rental cars should be reserved before arrival — inventory is limited. WiFi is available at the hotel. The island is small enough that you will quickly find your rhythm here. Most travelers who make the trip find that the quiet and the water are exactly what they came for.
