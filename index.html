Six Reasons to Stay Here

What actually
matters at this hotel.

01

Genuine oceanfront location

The property sits directly on Rota's waterfront in Songsong. The reef is audible from most rooms at night.

02

Everything within walking distance

Restaurants, dive operators, the harbor, and the post office are all within 10 minutes on foot. On a small island, that matters.

03

30+ years in operation

Family-owned and operated since 1993. Local decisions, local staff.

04

Rota is genuinely uncrowded

No cruise ships, no resort strips, no peak-season crowds. Rota gets a fraction of the visitors Guam and Saipan do.

05

Steps from the dive sites

Rota's reefs are among the best in the Pacific. The hotel is walking distance from the operators and directly above the water.

06

A quieter kind of Pacific island

Most visitors to the Pacific pass through Guam or Saipan. Rota sits apart — smaller, slower, and largely unchanged. That is increasingly rare.

The Island

Rota is not
on the way to anywhere.

No cruise ships. No resort strips. Some of the clearest water in the western Pacific — and almost no one in it. That is the point.
